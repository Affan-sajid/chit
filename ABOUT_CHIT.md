# About Chit

Chit is a minimal, fast note-taking app. Write, organize, and export your notes without clutter—everything lives in your browser.

## What you can do

- **Slash commands** — Type `/` to insert headings, lists, code blocks, and more
- **Rich text** — Bold, italic, code, bullet and numbered lists
- **Pages & sub-pages** — Create pages and nest them for a clear hierarchy
- **Page icons** — Add an emoji to any page from the icon picker
- **Drag & drop** — Reorder pages and move them between parents in the sidebar
- **Export** — Download a page as .txt, .md, or .pdf
- **Trash** — Deleted pages go to trash; restore or delete permanently
- **Import** — Bring in notes from the sidebar

## Quick start

Open the sidebar (or hover the left edge to peek), then click **+ New page** or press `Ctrl+N`. Start typing in the title or body, or type `/` to choose a block type.

## Shortcuts

- `Ctrl+N` — New page

Your data is stored locally. Built for writing—enjoy.
